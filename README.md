# Transform.io 🚀

**Transform.io** is a cutting-edge platform designed to revolutionize the way businesses manage their recruiting and customer relationships. By combining advanced AI-driven tools with an intuitive user interface, Transform.io bridges the gap between talent acquisition and customer relationship management.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

### 🧠 AI-Powered Resume Parsing
Say goodbye to manual data entry. Our intelligent parsing engine instantly extracts key skills, experiences, and contact details from resumes with high accuracy, automatically creating rich candidate profiles in seconds.

### 💼 Integrated ATS (Applicant Tracking System)
Streamline your hiring process from end to end. Track candidates across different pipeline stages, schedule interviews, and collaborate with your hiring team seamlessly all in one place.

### 🤝 Comprehensive CRM (Customer Relationship Management)
Manage your client relationships, track leads, and close deals faster. Our CRM module integrates smoothly with the ATS to ensure your recruitment team and sales team are always on the same page.

### 📊 Real-Time Analytics Dashboards
Make data-driven decisions with dynamic dashboards that provide actionable insights into hiring velocity, pipeline bottlenecks, and revenue forecasts.

### ⚙️ Automated Workflows
Eliminate repetitive tasks with customizable automation rules. Send automated email follow-ups, trigger status updates, and keep your pipelines moving effortlessly.

### 🌐 Multi-Industry Flexibility
Whether you're an agency, an executive manager, or an in-house recruiter in Tech or Healthcare, Transform.io adapts to your specific workflow and industry needs.

---

## 🛠️ Technology Stack
- **Frontend**: HTML5, Vanilla CSS (Modern CSS3 with custom variables & animations), Vanilla JavaScript.
- **Architecture**: Modular HTML pages generated via Python scripts, ensuring rapid deployment and easy maintenance.
