Typography

Primary font: 'SF Pro Display' (system fallback used where unavailable). Use font-weight and sizes consistent with the UI scale.

H1 — Page title / Hero

H2 — Section title

H3 — Card / Block title

Body copy example: Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet, consectetur adipiscing elit. Integer posuere erat a ante.

Spacing & Layout

Use consistent spacing tokens: small (8px), medium (16px), large (24-32px), and section padding 48-120px depending on context.

Do not alter the logo proportions. Keep UI elements aligned to the 8px grid where possible.
