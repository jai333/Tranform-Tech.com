Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

At Transform-Tech ("we", "our", or "us"), we are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website (transform-tech.com) and use our enterprise software services.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information about you in a variety of ways, including:

  • Personal Data: Name, email address, company name, and contact details provided when you register for an account or book a demo.
  • Authentication Data: When you sign in using third-party services like Google Workspace, we receive your email address and profile information in accordance with their authorization procedures.
  • Usage Data: Information automatically collected by our servers, such as your IP address, browser type, and interactions with our platform.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain our enterprise OS and services.
  • Process transactions and manage your account.
  • Send administrative information, such as updates, security alerts, and support messages.
  • Improve our platform's functionality and user experience.

3. Google Workspace Data Integration

If you connect your Google account to Transform-Tech:

  • We only request access to the specific data (like your email address and basic profile) necessary to authenticate you securely.
  • We do not share your Google user data with unauthorized third parties.
  • Transform-Tech's use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Data Security

We implement enterprise-grade security measures designed to protect your personal information. However, please be aware that no security measure is perfect or impenetrable, and no method of data transmission can be guaranteed against any interception or other type of misuse.
