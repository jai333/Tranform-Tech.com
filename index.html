Custom Applicant Tracking Systems

We build lightning-fast, intuitive applicant tracking systems that recruiters actually want to use. No more clunky, 15-click processes to move a candidate forward. We implement drag-and-drop Kanban boards natively.

"Our recruiters placed 30% more candidates in their first quarter on the Transform.io ATS. The friction is completely gone."

  • Automated Pipeline Sync Our engines map your entire candidate database against open job orders daily. If a matching candidate enters the system, our orchestrator immediately pushes their profile into the "Sourcing" Kanban lane.
  • React-Powered Visual Kanban We leverage React and Next.js for blazing fast load times. Recruiters can view 5,000+ candidate profiles in a single infinite scroll without browser lag, moving cards via zero-latency drag-and-drop.
  • Unified Candidate Timeline Every email, SMS, interview note, and resume version is consolidated into a single chronological timeline. If a recruiter leaves, the institutional knowledge stays permanently locked in the ATS.
  • Active Job Board Integrations Heuristic API packet inspection handles bi-directional syncing. We publish your jobs to LinkedIn and Indeed instantly, and we sandbox incoming applications, automatically parsing the PDFs into structured JSON profiles.
02 // CLIENT_MATRIX

Client CRM & Recruitment Automation

Track job orders, client communications, and margins automatically. We build workflows that automate the busywork so your team can focus on relationship building.

  • Cross-SaaS Workflow Triggers We connect independent SaaS applications. Example: A candidate is moved to "Offer Accepted" -> An AWS server triggers a background check -> An invoice is generated in QuickBooks -> A Slack webhook fires to the Account Manager.
  • Real-Time Probability Forecasting Predict your agency's revenue with real-time margin tracking and probability weighted pipelines. The system calculates exactly how much billing is expected to clear in Q4 based on historical interview-to-placement ratios.
  • Unattended Follow-Up Logic Zero-touch client management. We write custom node orchestration that deploys follow-up emails to hiring managers at exactly 9:00 AM on Monday, requesting interview feedback automatically without human intervention.
  • Immutable Client Data Silos Ensure your client list never leaves the building. We deploy role-based access control (RBAC) so junior recruiters only see the jobs they are assigned, preventing database exfiltration.
