02 // DEFENSE_MATRIX

Military-Grade Cybersecurity & Endpoint Management

Assume breach. That is our philosophy. We implement Zero-Trust Architectures that verify every single packet moving laterally through your network. We take complete control over your IT hardware fleet via unified endpoint management (UEM).

  • Continuous Vulnerability Sweeps (CVE) Our engines map your entire software inventory against national threat databases daily. If an unpatched server exists, our systems automatically isolate the node and deploy the hotfix silently.
  • Unified Endpoint Management (MDM) We push policies, software, and encryption keys to Windows, Mac, and Linux hardware completely over the air. A laptop is stolen? We remote-wipe the storage array via AES-256 destruction before it powers on.
  • Immutable Bare-Metal Backups Ransomware encrypts standard backups. We deploy airgapped, read-only "immutable" data archives. If your primary cluster is nuked, we can restore the entirety of your organization within 14 minutes.
  • Active Intrusion Prevention (IDS/IPS) Heuristic AI packet inspection. We drop DDoS attacks at the Cloudflare perimeter, and we sandbox internal malware execution in real-time, preventing lateral infection movement.
